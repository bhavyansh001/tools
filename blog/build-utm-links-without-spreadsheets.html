How to build UTM links without wrestling with spreadsheets

Use a dedicated URL builder to apply consistent source, medium, and campaign values, preview the finished address, and generate bulk links when needed.

September 27, 20264 min read

What are the essential UTM parameters?

  • utm_source identifies the platform or referrer, such as newsletter or linkedin.
  • utm_medium identifies the channel, such as email, social, or cpc.
  • utm_campaign groups links under one promotion or initiative.

Content and term parameters are optional and useful for distinguishing creative variants or paid-search keywords.

Why do campaign links become inconsistent?

Capitalization, spaces, naming changes, and one-off abbreviations fragment analytics reports. “LinkedIn,” “linkedin,” and “li” may appear as separate sources even though the marketer intended the same channel.

A repeatable naming approach

Choose lowercase names, use one separator, document approved channels, and keep campaign names short but recognizable. Preview the final URL before distributing it.

When should you generate links in bulk?

Bulk generation is useful when the destination or campaign remains constant but the source, placement, or creative changes. Exporting a consistent set is safer than manually editing a long query string for every placement.
